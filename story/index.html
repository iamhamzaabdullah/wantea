OUR STORY / JAKARTA

Good tea.
Better questions.

Wantea begins with a simple pleasure: a carefully made drink, and a moment to notice it.

Born in Jakarta, Wantea brings together tea, thoughtful design, curiosity and conversation. Our playful spirit has a serious intention: care for what goes into the cup, and make room for what comes out of the conversation.

We’re launching online with Tea Series 1, beginning with oolong. Karak Tea Series follows. Coffee, a physical café and organized discussions are part of the longer-term vision.
